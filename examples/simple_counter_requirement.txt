Implement a single-file Solidity 0.8.20 contract named SimpleCounter.

The contract must expose a public unsigned integer named count. Anyone may call
increment() to increase count by exactly one. Anyone may call decrement() to
decrease count by exactly one, but decrement() must revert when count is zero.
The contract must make no external calls, hold no Ether or tokens, and have no
owner, administrator, whitelist, or privileged role.
